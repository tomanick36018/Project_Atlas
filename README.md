## 🥗 Jouw Herstelprotocol voor Vandaag
*Volg deze stappen nauwkeurig op om je herstel te maximaliseren en blessures te voorkomen:*

### 📋 Stap 1: Beoordeling van de Training
The 10x400m run targeted running economy, neuromuscular power, and VO2max stimulation. By breaking down the distance into 400m blocks, you maintained high velocity and proper running mechanics despite the heat. This is excellent for 5km power transfer. Because it was a high-cadence workout, the eccentric load on your calves, Achilles tendons, and hamstrings was substantial.

### 🥛 Stap 2: Voeding & Hydratatie (Eten & Drinken)
Prioritize rehydration immediately: consume 500-750ml of water with an electrolyte tablet containing at least 500mg of sodium to replenish sweat losses. Within 45 minutes, consume a recovery shake or meal containing a 3:1 carbohydrate-to-protein ratio. Target 75g of fast-acting carbohydrates to restock depleted muscle glycogen and 25g of high-quality protein (whey or plant equivalent) to initiate muscle protein synthesis.

### 🧘‍♂️ Stap 3: Spieren & Mobiliteit (Stretching & Mobiliteit)
Focus recovery efforts on the posterior chain. Spend 10 minutes foam rolling your calves, hamstrings, and glutes to relieve high neuromuscular tension from the short intervals. Follow this with static stretching: 3x30 seconds of calf stretches (both straight-leg and bent-knee to target soleus), kneeling hip flexor stretches to open up the hips, and a gentle hamstring stretch. Avoid aggressive stretching if muscles feel overly tender.

### 🛌 Stap 4: Slaap & Vooruitblik naar Morgen
Prioritize sleep hygiene tonight by keeping your bedroom cool (around 18°C) to facilitate a drop in core body temperature, which may be elevated from the heat workout. Given your current TSB of -10.6 and today's high-intensity run, tomorrow must be a recovery or low-intensity active aerobic day. Expect either a light, low-intensity cycling commute or complete rest to allow your muscles to supercompensate for your next quality session.
